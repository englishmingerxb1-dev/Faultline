# ArcheAge Chronicles — C.A.S.I knowledge pack
Source: Wikily ArcheAge Chronicles wiki (https://wikily.gg/archeage-chronicles), gathered 22 Nov 2026.
The game is in Early Access, so details may change with patches.

## Overview
- Open-world action MMORPG by XLGAMES, set in the world of Auroria.
- Players arrive as pioneers attached to a trading company.
- Core activities: hunting, gathering, housing, farming, crafting, trade runs.
- Genres: Action, Adventure, Massively Multiplayer, RPG.
- Platform: PC via Steam. Early Access began 3 November 2026.
- Developer: XLGAMES (no separate publisher listed).

## Languages
- Official: English, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese. Full Korean voice audio.
- Machine translated: Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese.
- Change language: Options > General > Language.

## Servers and access
- A character stays on the server it was created on for the whole Early Access period (no transfers).
- Players on different servers cannot play together — groups must pick the same server.
- Steam Family Sharing is not supported.

## Classes and weapons
Four classes, two weapons each. Each weapon has its own skill tree, unlocked with Trait Points.
Roles below are inferred from the wiki's descriptions, not official labels.

### Warrior
- Longsword (starting weapon) — balanced melee, speed and strength, keen parries and decisive counters.
  Trait paths: drain enemy HP to boost own vitality, OR empowered parries for more refined combat.
- Greatsword — heavy, slow melee with crushing charged attacks and strong counters.
  Trait paths: weaken/neutralise enemies, OR shorter charge times and faster strikes.

### Berserker
- Axe (starting weapon) — frontline defender, steady defence and war cries that shield allies while counterattacking.
  Trait paths: boost ally survival, OR a more aggressive style with follow-up strikes.
- Warhammer — slow, wide area-damage swings hitting multiple targets.
  Trait paths: stronger charged attacks, OR skills that inflict Burn and Shock.

### Mage
- Elemental Staff (starting weapon) — ranged fire and lightning caster; charges skills for more damage, magic barriers for protection.
  Trait paths: retain previously cast skills to chain combos, OR channel arcane power to summon shooting stars.
- Wavesword — frost spellsword hybrid; creates ice crystals to chain skills and leaves sword afterimages.
  Trait paths: stronger Freeze to immobilise enemies, OR afterimage-based unconventional attacks.

### Ranger
- Hatchet (starting weapon) — fast close-range axe attacks, acrobatic movement and thrown weapons.
  Traits: combo skills that inflict Shock; stronger ranged attacks.
- Longbow — ranged sharpshooter, rapid shots, stays out of enemy reach.
  Traits: rapid shots inflict Burn over time; long-range attacks can inflict Freeze.

Status effects seen: Burn, Shock, Freeze.

## Companies (factions)
Five companies based in Auroria. Changing company costs 4,000 contribution (where listed); companies with listed ranks have 6 ranks.
- Bluesalt Brotherhood — Diamond Shores. Offers farm produce, forestry products, lumber, herbs. Motto: "Trades benefits all."
- Fairwind Federation — Whalesong Shores. Offers storage, trades, wagons, ships.
- Ironhart Covenant — Land of Gon. Offerings not listed on the wiki yet. Motto: "All veins are one!"
- Antiquary Society — Heartland. Offers alchemy, machining, relics, gems.
- Shadowhawk Syndicate — Vatic Shores. Offers minerals, leather, meat, bones.

## Regions mentioned
Diamond Shores, Whalesong Shores, Vatic Shores, Land of Gon, Heartland, Gylden Ruins, Astron Plains.

## Tempering (gear upgrades)
- Weapons go up to +15, armour up to +10. Each step costs Gold + Tempering Stones.
- A failed attempt does NOT destroy the item.
- Stones (separate weapon and armour versions): Faint (Uncommon, early steps), Vivid (Rare, middle), Brilliant (Epic, late).
- Success rates:
  - Weapon: +1 100%, +2 60%, +3 40%, +4 30%, +5 20%, +6 100%, +7 60%, +8 40%, +9 30%, +10 20%, +11 100%, +12 60%, +13 40%, +14 20%, +15 10%
  - Armour: +1 100%, +2 60%, +3 40%, +4 100%, +5 60%, +6 40%, +7 100%, +8 40%, +9 30%, +10 20%
  - The 100% steps (weapon +6, +11; armour +4, +7) are the points where the stone tier steps up.
- Cost examples: Common weapon +1 = 100 Gold + 1 Faint Weapon Stone; Common weapon +15 = 14,500 Gold + 5 Brilliant; Legendary weapon +15 = 1,093,000 Gold + 40 Brilliant. Costs rise sharply with step and rarity.
- Gear rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
